Robotic Drug-Analysis Platform

Hein Lab

Overview

I led mechanical and mechatronics development for a robotic drug-analysis platform designed to support BC's overdose crisis response. The system automated sample preparation workflows including gravimetric analysis, liquid transfer, centrifugation, and downstream analysis.

My Contributions

  • Led a 6-person engineering team developing automation hardware and controls.
  • Designed custom lab automation modules in SolidWorks and Fusion 360.
  • Built modules including a BLDC centrifuge, syringe-pump liquid handler, decapper, vial gripper, and transfer fixtures.
  • Integrated Python workflow control with C++/ROS2 hardware automation and computer-vision error detection.

Tools Used

SolidWorks, Fusion 360, GD&T, ROS2, C++, Python, KiCAD, 3D printing, PCB design

Impact

Reduced automation module costs from approximately $1,500-$5,000 to under $100 while achieving reliable robotic sample-handling workflows.
