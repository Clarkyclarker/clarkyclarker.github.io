Calibra: Robotic Platform for Automated Drug Checking and Quantification

Why Calibra?

Each year, thousands of people in British Columbia experience overdoses, many linked to an increasingly unpredictable and contaminated drug supply. Drug-checking services can help reduce this risk by identifying what is actually in a submitted sample, but higher-quality analysis is often limited by the need for time-consuming manual preparation, specialized equipment, and trained personnel. Developed with support from University of British Columbia and BC provincial health authorities, Calibra addresses this bottleneck by automating sample preparation using a robotic arm, custom electromechanical modules, smart sample tracking, and plug-and-play Python-based control.

My Contributions

  • Led a 6-person engineering team developing automation hardware and controls.
  • Designed custom lab automation modules in SolidWorks and Fusion 360.
  • Built modules including a BLDC centrifuge, syringe-pump liquid handler, decapper, vial gripper, and transfer fixtures.
  • Integrated Python workflow control with C++/ROS2 hardware automation and computer-vision error detection.

Tools Used

SolidWorks, Fusion 360, GD&T, ROS2, C++, Python, KiCAD, 3D printing, PCB design

Impact

Reduced automation module costs from approximately $1,500–$5,000 to under $100 while achieving reliable robotic sample-handling workflows.
